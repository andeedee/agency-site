Our Story

Built from the same frustration you're feeling right now

Amplo Labs wasn't born in a boardroom. It started when our founder, Andy Dutson, watched a thriving local business lose thousands of leads every month — not because their offer was weak, but because no one was following up fast enough.

The tools existed. The data was clear. But the gap between knowing and doing felt impossible to close without a team no small business could afford. So we built the bridge ourselves.

Today we deploy the same AI systems used by enterprise sales teams — stripped of the complexity, sized for growing businesses, and engineered to pay for themselves in the first month.

"Every lead you don't follow up in five minutes is a lead your competitor is closing."

— Andy Dutson, Founder
